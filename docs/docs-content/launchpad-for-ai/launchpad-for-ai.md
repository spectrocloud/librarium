Launchpad for AI is a turnkey AI appliance that enables you to deploy and run large language models (LLMs) entirely
on-premises. It is a standalone product for teams that need local AI inference without cloud dependency.

## Why Launchpad for AI?

Enterprises operating in regulated industries, air-gapped environments, or regions with strict data residency
requirements often cannot send sensitive data to external AI services. Launchpad for AI brings the model to your
infrastructure, not the other way around.
