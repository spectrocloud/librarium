When the appliance serves HTTPS with a certificate issued by its own platform Certificate Authority (CA), the connect
panel offers that CA certificate as a download. Your machine does not trust the platform CA yet, so download the
certificate before you configure the agent.

1. In the console, open the **Connect a coding agent** panel. Select **Connect Coding Agent** on the homepage, select
   **Connect a Coding Agent** in the **Create API token** dialog that reveals a new token, or select **Connect Agent**
   in the dialog that finishes the **Add client** wizard.

2. Select the tab for your agent, and then select **CA certificate**. The browser saves
   `palette-ai-inference-launchpad-ca.crt` to your `Downloads` folder.

If the panel shows no **CA certificate** step, the appliance does not need one. Skip this section and every later step
that points at the downloaded file.
