:::info

After upgrading {props.edition} to 4.9.a, the `ingress.type` and `ingress.ingress.internal` fields have been removed
from the Helm chart. If you have either field in your `values.yaml` override file, the upgrade ignores them silently and no action is required for the upgrade to succeed. We recommend removing both fields from your override file as
recommended hygiene.

:::
