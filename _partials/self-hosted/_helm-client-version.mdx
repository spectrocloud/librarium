:::warning

**Use Helm client v3.14.0 or later.** Older Helm clients do not wait for pre-upgrade hook resources to finish
terminating before recreating them. When you run `helm upgrade` against the `hubble` chart with a client earlier than
v3.14.0, the `hubble-system` namespace can be inadvertently deleted mid-upgrade, which results in **loss of MongoDB
data** — including tenants and cluster metadata — with no automatic recovery.

The fix for this hook behavior was released in
[Helm v3.14.0](https://github.com/helm/helm/releases/tag/v3.14.0) for the underlying change). Confirm your client version
with `helm version` before you continue.

:::
