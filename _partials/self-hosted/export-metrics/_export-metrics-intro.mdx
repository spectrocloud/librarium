Self-hosted {props.version} can export a curated set of platform metrics to your existing observability stack using an
OpenTelemetry collector. You configure and manage the export from the **Metrics** tab under **Administration** in the
system console, without deploying a customer-managed agent.

The **Metrics** tab also controls which signals {props.version} collects from your managed clusters. Those collection
settings are outside the scope of this page, which covers exporting the collected metrics.

Export is available for Helm-based self-hosted installations, and works in both connected and airgapped environments as
long as the destination endpoint is reachable.

{/* Scope: covers self-hosted Palette and VerteX (Helm-based) only. Appliance support is a separate epic. Enterprise Cluster (EC) is not supported. */}

:::info

The export sends metrics only. It never exports logs, traces, or audit events, and {props.version} adds no dashboards or
alerts on top of what you export. Workload cluster observability is configured per cluster, not from the system console.

:::

The Splunk HTTP Event Collector (HEC) is the supported export destination.
