Self-hosted {props.version} can export a curated set of platform metrics to your existing observability stack using an
OpenTelemetry collector. You configure and manage the export from the **Metrics** tab in the system console, without
deploying a customer-managed agent.

Export is available for Helm and management appliance installations, and works in both connected and airgapped
environments as long as the destination endpoint is reachable.

{/* TODO(PEM-8670): Confirm Enterprise Cluster (EC) installs are out of scope for export-metrics, even though the image pull secret upgrade note references the EC system console. */}

:::info

The export sends metrics only. It never exports logs, traces, or audit events, and {props.version} adds no dashboards or
alerts on top of what you export. Workload cluster observability is configured per cluster, not from the system console.

:::
